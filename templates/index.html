What is AQI?

The Air Quality Index (AQI) is an official public health scale (0 to 500) that reports how clean or polluted ambient outdoor air is each hour.

Why It Matters to You

Higher numbers mean greater air toxicity. When AQI exceeds 100, sensitive people experience coughing and throat irritation; above 200 affects everyone.

How AirGuard Helps

Instead of just showing numbers, we provide actionable steps: whether to send children to outdoor play, wear an N95 mask, or run indoor air purifiers.

Standard Public Health Scale

The 6 AQI Categories Explained

Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and WHO color-coded classification system for everyday citizens.

0 - 50

Good

Clean air with zero health risk. Ideal for all outdoor exercises.

51 - 100

Satisfactory

Minor breathing discomfort to unusually sensitive individuals.

101 - 200

Moderate

Discomfort to children, elderly, and people with lung/heart disease.

201 - 300

Poor

Breathing discomfort on prolonged exposure. Wear N95 masks outdoors.

301 - 400

Very Poor

Respiratory illness risk. Vulnerable groups must stay strictly indoors.

401 - 500+

Severe

Emergency! Affects healthy people. Seal windows and run air purifiers.
